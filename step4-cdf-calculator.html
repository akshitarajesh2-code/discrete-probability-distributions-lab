Binomial PMF Calculator

Evaluates $P(X = k)$

PMF
Mathematical Expression
P(X=k) = C(n,k) p^k (1-p)^(n-k)
Exact Probability
P(X = 7) = 0.1844
Interpretation: This represents the exact probability of obtaining exactly 7 successes out of 20 independent trials.

Binomial CDF Calculator

Evaluates $P(X \le k)$ and $P(X > k)$

CDF
Cumulative Probability P(X ≤ k)
P(X ≤ 7) = 0.6010
Complement Probability P(X > k) = 1 - P(X ≤ k)
P(X > 7) = 0.3990

P(X ≤ k) means the probability of getting k or fewer successes.

Here, obtaining 7 or fewer successes has a probability of 0.6010 (60.10%), while getting more than 7 successes is 0.3990 (39.90%).
