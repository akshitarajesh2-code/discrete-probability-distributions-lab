Binomial Distribution Module

Configure $n$, $p$, and $k$ to observe real-time probability distributions, statistics, and graphs.

Formula: $P(X = k) = \binom{n}{k} p^k (1-p)^{n-k}$

Probability Mass Function (PMF)

Exact Successes
P(X = k) = C(n,k) · pk · (1-p)n-k
P(X = 7) = 0.1844
Meaning: The probability of obtaining exactly 7 successes in 20 trials with success probability 0.35 is 0.1844 (18.44%).

Cumulative & Complementary CDF

Cumulative
Cumulative P(X ≤ 7): 0.6010
Complement P(X > 7) = 1 - P(X ≤ k): 0.3990

Explanation: P(X ≤ k) means the probability of getting k or fewer successes.

In this setup, P(X ≤ 7) is 0.6010, while P(X > 7) is 0.3990.
